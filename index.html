Research and context

Some traditional Christian and Islamic interpretations share moral objections to abortion, same-sex marriage, and certain views about sex and gender. That does not mean all Muslims or Christians agree, or that personal moral opposition necessarily implies support for legal enforcement.

Pew Research Center: The World's Muslims (2013) surveys moral and religious attitudes across Muslim populations in multiple countries. PRRI research includes American Christian nationalism and policy attitudes. These sources are not identical surveys and do not establish a statistical correlation for this quiz.

Satire disclaimer and limitations

This is a satirical reflection exercise, not scientific research, a religious identity test, or a validated assessment. The questions intentionally contrast Islamic and Christian religious authority. Different responses do not establish a person's motives, hypocrisy, or attitudes toward Muslims. Views on education, abortion, gender, and marriage vary within both religions. No responses are collected.
